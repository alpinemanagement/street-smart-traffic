Street Smart Traffic Dispatch

Street Smart Traffic Dispatch is a secure workforce management platform designed to support traffic management operations. The platform helps dispatch teams coordinate projects, schedule employees, manage work assignments, communicate with field staff, and streamline daily operations.

Key Features

  • Employee scheduling and dispatch
  • Project and client management
  • Work assignment tracking
  • Team communication and notifications
  • Secure document management
  • Administrative reporting and operational tools

Secure Sign-In

Users authenticate using secure identity providers, including Google Sign-In, to access their authorized accounts. Authentication is handled securely through our identity management system.

Intended Users

Street Smart Traffic Dispatch is intended for authorized employees, subcontractors, dispatchers, supervisors, and administrators of Street Smart Traffic and affiliated organizations.

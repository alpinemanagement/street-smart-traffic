Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 7, 2026

Street Smart Traffic Dispatch ("the Application") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

When you use the Application, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture (when provided by your identity provider)
  • Authentication information required to securely sign you in
  • Work-related information associated with your account
  • Documents and files that you upload
  • System logs and usage information used to maintain the security and reliability of the Application

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using Google, we receive basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture. This information is used solely to authenticate your identity, create or access your account, and personalize your experience within the Application.

We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, or any other Google data unless explicitly stated and authorized.

How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account
  • Provide access to the Application
  • Manage scheduling and dispatch operations
  • Facilitate communication between authorized users
  • Improve the reliability, performance, and security of the Application
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only:

  • With authorized administrators of your organization
  • With trusted service providers that support the operation of the Application
  • When required by applicable law or legal process

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the services, meet operational requirements, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
